Our Story

A passion for food that started in a small kitchen and grew into a community favorite.

Welcome to Savor & Spice

Founded in 2015 by Chef Maria Rodriguez, Savor & Spice began as a dream to bring bold, globally inspired flavors to our neighborhood. What started as a cozy 20-seat bistro has blossomed into a beloved dining destination.

Every dish on our menu tells a story — from family recipes passed down through generations to innovative creations born in our kitchen. We believe great food starts with great ingredients, which is why we partner with local farms and suppliers.

Whether you're dining in, picking up, or ordering delivery, we're committed to delivering an unforgettable experience with every meal.
